# Regional crab grounds

The 69 gameplay grounds are fictional, inspired by the user's approximate regional map. They are not survey results, legal management boundaries or live stock forecasts. Real closures, quotas and jurisdictional limits are not simulated. In particular, the western Tanner region follows the supplied game map rather than claiming a commercially surveyed Russian fishing area.

| Region | Fields | Main species |
|---|---:|---|
| Aleutians | 12 | Golden king |
| Pribilof | 7 | Red king (4) / blue king (3) |
| Eastern Bering | 10 | Tanner / bairdi |
| Bristol Bay | 6 | Red king |
| Bering Sea | 16 | Snow / opilio |
| Western Bering | 12 | Tanner / bairdi |
| St Matthew | 6 | Blue king |

Reymonds spot remains a high-potential Bristol Bay field. UNALASKA SHELF remains the accessible first field. Every field is 2× or 3× the old ellipse's linear dimensions (4× or 9× its area), with matching abundance/core extents. Water clipping, water-only migration, sonar, knowledge forecasts, direction arrows and rank-V cores use the same data. Species is recorded when new pots are deployed; legacy pots infer species from their location. Existing deployed gear and fleet targets are retained; new fleet trips choose the revised fields. Existing recorded hold values are preserved. Species appear in chart reports and haul messages; the cargo price remains a weighted aggregate, not a per-species inventory system.

## Price benchmarks

These are static gameplay reference prices per kg of whole landed crab, not retail legs/meat, live market quotes, or claims that all regions are currently open. Different reference years are intentional because comparable recent blue-king data are scarce. Convert USD/lb ÷ 0.45359237, round to cents/kg, then use the game's fixed 10 SEK/USD accounting. The pre-existing 1–5× voyage bonus and skill bonuses remain separate game mechanics.

Fishing Intelligence quotes the current gross price at each fishing ground. In multiplayer it uses the shared world clock, the same as haul valuation. Cargo & Finances shows a local estimate when no crab is aboard and otherwise a weight-adjusted average of the catch booked at haul time. Both quotes include the current vessel's sale bonuses and exclude loan repayment or rare-catch bonuses. Moving grounds and distance from port can affect what an individual haul earns; a market change does not reprice crab already in the hold.
